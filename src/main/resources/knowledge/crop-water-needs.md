## Why timing matters more than totals

Crops are not equally sensitive to water shortage throughout the season. A shortage during
flowering or early yield formation costs far more yield than the same shortage during vegetative
growth or ripening. FAO expresses this with the yield response factor Ky: the relative yield loss
for a given relative shortfall in crop water use. A Ky above 1 means yield falls faster than water
use, so the crop is sensitive. Seasonal values are about 1.25 for maize, 1.1 for potato, 1.05 for
tomato, 1.05 to 1.15 for wheat and 0.85 for soybean and cotton.

## Maize

Maize is most sensitive to water stress around tasselling and silking (flowering): stress then
reduces pollination and kernel set and cannot be recovered later. Grain filling is the next most
sensitive stage. Moderate stress during early vegetative growth is tolerated fairly well.
FAO-56 suggests a depletion fraction p of about 0.55 and a mid-season Kc of about 1.20, with roots
reaching 1.0 to 1.7 m.

## Tomato

Tomato is most sensitive during flowering and fruit set, and needs steady water during fruit
enlargement: irregular watering (dry spells followed by heavy irrigation) is linked to blossom-end
rot and fruit cracking. Water is often reduced slightly as fruit ripens to improve quality. FAO-56
suggests p of about 0.40 (irrigate before 40 % of available water is used) and a mid-season Kc of
about 1.15.

## Potato

Potatoes are shallow-rooted (about 0.4 to 0.6 m) and sensitive, especially from tuber initiation
through tuber bulking. Stress then reduces the number and size of tubers and can cause growth
cracks and misshapen tubers when water returns. Keep depletion low: FAO-56 suggests p of about 0.35.

## Wheat and other small grains

Wheat is most sensitive from booting through flowering, then during early grain fill. Water use
falls sharply as the crop ripens, and late irrigation adds little. FAO-56 suggests p of about 0.55.

## Leafy vegetables and onions

Leafy crops (cabbage, lettuce) need consistent moisture for leaf quality; p is around 0.45 for
cabbage. Onions are very shallow-rooted with p around 0.30 and need frequent, light irrigations,
stopping before harvest so bulbs cure.
